02 / OUR STORY

OUR
STORY.

Majan Cooling began its journey in 1996, providing air-conditioning services with a focus on practical solutions, quality workmanship and customer satisfaction.

Over more than three decades, the company has grown from its early roots in Oman into a service provider extending its AC expertise to customers across Oman and the UAE.

Our growth has been driven by a simple approach: understand the customer's requirement, provide the right solution and complete every job with care and responsibility.

We focus on quality products, dependable components and professional workmanship while keeping our services accessible and reasonably priced.

More than 30 years later, our goal remains the same — keeping homes and businesses comfortable with reliable air-conditioning solutions.
